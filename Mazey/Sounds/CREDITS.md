# Sound credits

**Applause.m4a** is "Well Done" by qubodup, released under CC0 (public domain):
https://opengameart.org/content/well-done

It was converted to mono AAC at 44.1 kHz, with a short fade-out at the end.
Everything else the app plays is synthesized in code (`SoundSynth`) and uses no audio files.
