How it works

Finish mazes.
Collect stuff.

No timers, no losing, no pressure. Just a maze to solve and something nice at the end.

👆

Drag to the finish

Slide a finger along the path. The character glides through the corridors, with a little melody that plays as it goes.

⭐

Collect stars

Pick up the stars along the way. Stars add up forever and unlock new characters. Nothing is spent and nothing is lost.

📒

Fill the sticker book

Every maze finished earns a sticker from its world, 88 in all. Tap one that's missing to see where to find it.

🏎️

Unlock characters

21 characters, from a race car and a rocket to a panda and a dragon, can be played instead of the world's own.

💡

Hints when stuck

A tap on the light bulb shows a small hint, and more taps show more. It fades away by itself.

🎹

Music while you play

Classical tunes play note by note as the path grows, and there is applause at the end. Each sound can be switched off.

Mazes that grow with them

Five levels.
Four shapes.

Start with a tiny maze and work up to big winding ones. Mazes come as squares, circles, hexagons and triangles, and always fit the screen, in any orientation, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

For parents

Calm by design.

Mazey is made to be the kind of game you're happy to hand over. There's nothing to tap that leads out of the app or costs money.

  • No ads and no in-app purchases
  • No accounts, no tracking, no analytics
  • Works fully offline, with no network access
  • No daily rewards, streak pressure or timers shown to kids
  • Progress stays on the device
  • A simple math question guards the reset button
  • A statistics page for grown-ups: mazes finished and how long they take
  • Free and open source
