Privacy Policy

Mazey collects nothing.

Mazey is made for young children, so it is built to know as little as possible. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no network access.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

The short version

  • No data collection. The developer receives no information about you or your child.
  • No ads and no tracking. Mazey includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
  • No network. The app works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.
  • No purchases. There are no in-app purchases and no links that leave the app.

What is stored on the device

To remember progress, Mazey saves a small amount of data on the device itself: the stickers earned, stars collected, the chosen character, settings such as sound switches and difficulty, and a list of finished mazes with how long each took (shown on the grown-ups' Statistics page).

This data never leaves the device. It is removed when the app is deleted, or when a grown-up uses Reset everything in Settings.

Permissions

Mazey does not ask for access to your location, photos, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal information.

Children's privacy

Mazey does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It is designed to be safe to hand to a young child, with no ads, no links to the web, and no way to buy anything.

Sounds

All sounds are generated inside the app, except the applause, which is a short public-domain (CC0) recording. No audio is ever recorded or sent anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the new version will be posted on this page and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through GitHub Issues.
